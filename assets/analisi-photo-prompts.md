# Foto illustrative delle due analisi

Generate con lo strumento imagegen integrato; esportate in WebP per la landing.

## analisi-reputazione.webp

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: editorial photograph for an Italian dental practice landing page, illustrating reputation analysis
Primary request: A dentist and a female front-desk colleague in a contemporary Italian dental studio, calmly reviewing patient feedback together on a tablet after appointments.
Scene/backdrop: real reception area of a modern but approachable dental practice, warm off-white walls, natural materials, no visible brand.
Style/medium: candid high-end commercial photography, believable people and skin texture, not staged stock-photo smiles.
Composition/framing: landscape 4:3, medium shot with people and tablet clearly visible, enough safe crop at edges for responsive layouts.
Lighting/mood: soft daylight, warm-neutral palette harmonizing with cream, charcoal and subtle coral accents.
Constraints: no readable words, no logos, no watermarks, no graphic overlays, no medical procedure, no visible sensitive patient information.
```
